What to expect

Our Sunday morning begins with Sunday School, followed by a worship service with congregational singing, prayer, and a message from God's Word.

Come as you are. You'll find a friendly, family-oriented congregation, and someone will be glad to show you around.

Who we are

Mt Zion House of Prayer is a Mennonite congregation near Utica, Ohio. We believe in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior, the authority of Scripture, and following Jesus in everyday life, in our homes, our work, and our community.

Come for a visit!

Whether you've followed Jesus for years or are just curious, you're welcome at Mt Zion. Join us Sunday morning for Sunday School and worship.
